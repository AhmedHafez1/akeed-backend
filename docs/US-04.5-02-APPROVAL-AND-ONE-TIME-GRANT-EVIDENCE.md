## Corrected foundation defect

`CreditAccountingRepository.checkInvariant` built its ledger and reservation subqueries with an unqualified outer column, because Drizzle renders a column reference inside a select-list `sql` fragment without its table name. The emitted predicate was `WHERE org_id = org_id` — a tautology — so every account's report carried the sum of every organization's ledger.

The observable effect is that `lockAccount` rejects every credit mutation as an invariant mismatch as soon as a second organization holds any ledger row. The approval transaction reproduced it on its second organization. Both subqueries now alias their table and qualify the outer reference, and `test/credit-foundation.contract-spec.ts` gained "scopes the invariant report to the requested organization", which fails against the previous implementation.

## Approval contract

`POST /api/admin/standalone-billing/approvals/apply` runs one serializable transaction per organization, retried at most three times on `40001`/`40P01`, in this lock order:

1. `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended(ownerUserId, 0))` for each owner — the same key `StandaloneOrganizationProvisioningRepository.provision` takes, so staff approval and merchant self-signup cannot interleave.
2. `FOR UPDATE` on `organizations`, then `memberships`, then `integrations`.
3. `ensurePendingAccount` then `CreditAccountingRepository.lockAccount`, which acquires `FOR UPDATE` on `credit_accounts` and rejects an inconsistent projection.

Later credit mutations (US-04.5-03/04) must take the account lock in this position.

Inside that transaction the approval re-loads the snapshot, compares the preview fingerprint, and then writes, in order: the Standalone source if absent, the transitional Starter/`not_required` entitlement, `free_grant +30` under `standalone-free-grant:<orgId>:v1` with the staff actor id, the account update carrying `status='active'` plus `approved_by`/`approved_at`/`approval_reason` in the same versioned statement, an invariant re-check, and the `standalone-billing.approve` audit row. The audit insert is a commit precondition: a failure there rolls the grant back.

Exactly-once is enforced by the database in two independent ways — `credit_ledger_free_grant_key` (one `free_grant` row per organization) and `credit_ledger_idempotency_key` on `(org_id, idempotency_key)`. Application-level replay is additionally short-circuited by a prior-audit probe on `(userId, previewId, orgId)`, which returns `already_applied` with the original audit id.

The preview fingerprint is SHA-256 over the whole snapshot, which now includes account status, balances, version and whether a free grant is already posted. Any credit-side drift between preview and apply invalidates the preview.

## Eligibility vocabulary

`eligible | already_approved | skipped | ambiguous`, with reasons `create_source`, `activate_source`, `already_approved`, `organization_missing`, `native_source`, `native_billing_history`, `owner_missing`, `multiple_owners`, `multiple_owned_organizations`, `source_conflict`, `source_inactive`, `billing_conflict`, `accounting_anchor_missing`, `account_missing` and `account_suspended`. Apply outcomes are `approved | already_applied | unchanged | skipped | changed | failed`.

The pilot's conflict guards are unchanged: a Shopify or other native source, retained native billing history, ambiguous ownership or unexplained billing fields is reported, never converted. `already_approved` is now decided by an active account or a posted free grant rather than by plan entitlement, so a migration-cohort organization that already carries the Starter columns stays eligible for its one launch grant.

## Configuration and rollout

`STANDALONE_CREDIT_APPROVAL_ENABLED` (default `false`) gates apply only and replaces the retired `STANDALONE_PILOT_ACTIVATION_ENABLED`. Preview and the account listing remain available while it is off; apply fails closed with `403 STANDALONE_APPROVAL_DISABLED` before the preview is read. `ADMIN_CONTROL_TOWER_ENABLED` (404 when off) and `ADMIN_REQUIRE_AAL2` are unchanged and still apply.

`STANDALONE_CREDIT_BILLING_ENABLED` is the separate switch that decides where the entitlement grant happens:

| Flag | Provisioning | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `false` (default) | Writes Starter/`not_required`/anchor as today, plus a pending credit account | Writes the entitlement and the credit grant; the gate is inert |
| `true` | Writes only the pending credit account; the billing columns stay NULL | Writes the entitlement, activates the account and posts the launch grant |

The Starter write inside approval is transitional. US-04.5-03 removes it once `UsageAccountingRouter` routes Standalone sends through the credit ledger; the two stories are deployed together.

Rollout: deploy with both flags off, review the account listing, enable `STANDALONE_CREDIT_BILLING_ENABLED` and `STANDALONE_CREDIT_APPROVAL_ENABLED` for a reviewed cohort, approve in bounded batches of at most 50, then return the apply flag to `false`. On anomaly, disable further apply. Never delete a committed grant or reset merchant history as a rollback.

## Denial behaviour

`CreditApprovalService` treats the credit account row as the subscription to prepaid billing: an organization that holds one must have it `active` before any billable action, and an organization without one keeps its plan entitlement. That keeps the check free of platform branching, which the E02 core boundary gate enforces.

| Surface | Behaviour while approval is pending |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /api/onboarding/complete` | `409 STANDALONE_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` with `approvalStatus`; state reports the `approval_required` blocker |
| Manual order create and verification retry | `409 STANDALONE_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` |
| Test verification | `409 STANDALONE_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` |
| Order eligibility and the send path | skipped with reason `standalone_approval_required` |

`standalone_approval_required` joins `RETRYABLE_VERIFICATION_REASONS`, `BLOCKED_EVENT_REASONS` and `SEND_FAILURE_REASONS`, so withheld work resumes once approval commits. Reading existing orders, verifications, dispatch history and settings is untouched.

Until US-04.5-03 these are advisory checks layered over the existing entitlement rules, not the accounting authority. The transactional authority arrives with the dispatch-claim work.

## Disposable PostgreSQL rehearsal

Executed against Docker PostgreSQL 17 instances bound to loopback ports only, with synthetic disposable credentials. `test/standalone-approval.contract-spec.ts` builds a randomized schema, replays `0027_standalone_pilot_permissions.sql`, and hand-builds the `credit_accounts` and `credit_ledger_entries` DDL from `0032` including the free-grant partial unique index, the projection/sign/source checks, the ledger immutability trigger and the account version guard. It does not replay all of `0032`, which needs the payment and dispatch tables this story does not touch.

Eleven tests cover: activation with the launch grant and preserved monthly usage/anchor/audit count; a repeat preview reporting `already_approved` without a second grant; four concurrent approvals of one organization yielding one `approved`, one ledger row and `version = 1`; audit failure rolling back the grant, the account and the source; stale preview after a membership change; preview drift after a credit-account change; a batch where one organization drifts and the other still commits; Shopify-source and multiple-owner organizations never converted; concurrent staff approval and merchant signup converging on one source and one grant; and authenticated writes to the ledger denied with `42501`.

## Deployment and recovery

1. Deploy with `STANDALONE_CREDIT_BILLING_ENABLED=false` and `STANDALONE_CREDIT_APPROVAL_ENABLED=false`. The staff route moves to `/api/admin/standalone-billing`; the old `/api/admin/standalone-pilots` routes no longer exist, so deploy both projects together.
2. Review the account listing and the invariant checker before enabling anything. Resolve every `account_missing`, `source_conflict` or `billing_conflict` row through the documented support process rather than by editing data.
3. Enable both flags for the allowlisted cohort, approve in small batches, and keep the downloaded preview and results reports.
4. On rollback, disable apply and revert the application release. Keep all accounts, ledger rows, audit rows, sources, orders and verification history. A committed grant is never deleted; a correction is a later audited `staff_adjustment`, which US-04.5-06 introduces.
