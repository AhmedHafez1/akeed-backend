# US-04.5-07 implementation evidence

Date: 2026-09-10

## Delivered

- Migration `0034_billing_observability_and_settlements.sql` (registered in `drizzle/meta/_journal.json`) and the matching Drizzle schema:
  - `billing_reconciliation_runs`: unique `run_key`, trigger (`nightly`, `settlement`, `manual`), mode (`local_only`, `report_only`, `active`), status, timestamps, and aggregate counts. A settlement run must name its report.
  - `billing_reconciliation_attempts`: immutable per-target outcomes, unique on `(run_id, target_kind, target_key)`, with a tenant-safe `(purchase_id, org_id)` foreign key.
  - `billing_reconciliation_findings`: deduplicated by SHA-256 fingerprint, with severity, open/resolved status, first/last seen, occurrence and retry counts, next action, next attempt, and sanitized context.
  - `billing_settlement_reports`: immutable (update/delete/truncate trigger), append-only corrections through a single `supersedes_id` per row, unique revision per provider report, and a unique `(actor_id, idempotency_key)`.
  - Service-role-only RLS on all four tables, plus queue, organization, and retention indexes.
- A dedicated `billing-reconciliation` BullMQ queue. `upsertJobScheduler` registers one nightly scheduler (`30 2 * * *`, `Africa/Cairo`). One processor runs at concurrency 1. Nightly runs are keyed to their job (`nightly:<job timestamp>`), and manual and settlement runs to a durable run id (`billing-reconciliation-<runId>` job id), so every retry continues the same run.
- `BillingObservabilityService.processRun`:
  - scans accounts through `readReconciliation` and `findContradictions` in keyset pages of 50;
  - scans static provider/ledger signals;
  - scans purchase candidates (flagged or stale pending of any age; successful, refunded, or disputed changed within seven days), also in pages of 50;
  - compares an effective settlement report when the run names one.
  
  Each completed target writes an attempt only after its findings persist. A resumed run therefore skips that target without making its findings look unseen. A purchase whose inquiry finished before an interruption is never asked about again in that run.
- `PaymentReconciliationService.reconcile({ scheduled, reportOnly })`. It still makes exactly one bounded inquiry per call. Report-only mode returns the normalized fact without ingesting or writing the purchase, including when the provider call throws. Active mode passes every fact through `PaymentCallbackService.ingest`. Merchant and forced-staff behaviour is unchanged. The trusted-field comparison is one shared policy, `paymentEventMismatch`, used by both ingestion and report-only comparison.
- Detectors:
  - provider success without grant: from contradictions, and from a report-only inquiry that finds an ungranted success;
  - grant without verified success;
  - duplicate provider identifiers across purchases;
  - stale pending;
  - reference, amount, currency, integration, mode, or ownership mismatch: from quarantined events and from report-only inquiry;
  - refund/dispute discrepancies: from contradictions and provider refund totals;
  - posted-balance and held-reservation projection drift;
  - contradictory source rows;
  - exact settlement count and minor-unit differences, including the report's own arithmetic;
  - debt, refund, and chargeback states;
  - deferred provider inquiries;
  - provider slowness and error rate;
  - backlog size and age.
- Poison handling: a deferred scheduled inquiry opens `provider_inquiry_deferred` with a retry count and `nextAttemptAt`. The backoff starts at 15 minutes, doubles per failure, and is capped at 24 hours. Later runs leave Paymob alone until the finding is due.
- A completed full scan resolves findings it did not see. A failed run resolves nothing.
- Product and finance metrics come from immutable purchases and ledger rows:
  - Liability allocation spends free grants and positive staff adjustments first. Negative adjustments reduce non-cash and then paid batches. Failure reversals restore the source consumption's allocation. Refunds, chargebacks, and reinstatements act on their original paid batch, valued at that purchase's unit price.
  - Net revenue, fees, VAT, ARPPU, and revenue per accepted message are `null` unless effective settlement reports cover the whole requested bounded period.
- Structured events:
  - `standalone-billing-alert` from reconciliation, the callback path (`trusted_data_mismatch`, `callback_failure`, `projection_mismatch`, `duplicate_grant_attempt`), and the Paymob HMAC guard (`invalid_hmac`);
  - `standalone-billing-metric-summary`, which carries counters only.
  
  Redaction now also covers card/wallet numbers and tokens, MSISDN, and Paymob `secret_key`, `hmac_secret`, and `private_key` field names, in addition to the evidence, raw payload, phone, and email keys.
- Configuration: the twelve `STANDALONE_BILLING_*` observability variables, validated at startup with the defaults in [ENVIRONMENT.md](ENVIRONMENT.md). The scheduled-inquiry switch affects scheduled inquiry only.
- Staff API under `/api/admin/standalone-billing`, all `private, no-store`:
  - `GET /health`, `GET /reconciliation/findings`, and `GET /settlements` use the existing staff/AAL2 guard.
  - `POST /reconciliation/runs` and `POST /settlements` (which requires `Idempotency-Key`) also need the operations flag and a named operator. They are throttled, carry the request id, and are audited. An accepted settlement queues `settlement:<id>` immediately.
- Frontend (Standalone billing staff page):
  - health, last run, rollout mode, backlog, and provider performance;
  - 7, 30, and 90-day and all-time presets;
  - product and finance cards with a settlement-coverage warning;
  - scroll-contained findings queue with status, severity, and code filters and account links;
  - named-operator run control;
  - EGP settlement entry and append-only correction;
  - read-only states for staff who are not named operators.
  
  Account detail lists that organization's open findings with their prescribed existing action. All copy is in English and Arabic.
- [Operations runbook](STANDALONE_BILLING_OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md): reconciliation runs, settlement entry and correction, outage response (callback/HMAC, callback database, Paymob timeout/rate limit, Redis, PostgreSQL, stale pending, trusted mismatch, projection mismatch, refund/chargeback/debt), Railway alerts by `alertCode`, retention, and credential rotation.

## Decisions and assumptions

1. **Report-only and scheduled inquiry are separate switches** (per the plan). `STANDALONE_BILLING_SCHEDULED_INQUIRY_ENABLED=false` keeps all local checks running. `STANDALONE_BILLING_RECONCILIATION_REPORT_ONLY=true` stays on until comparisons are clean.
2. **A redelivered `running` run is claimable.** The run is 1:1 with its BullMQ job, so redelivery means the holder stalled or died. Completed-attempt markers make continuing safe.
3. **A deferred inquiry resolves only while scheduled inquiry is on.** Turning the switch off must not make it look resolved.
4. **Settlement periods are exact instants.** The UI enters local time and submits UTC ISO timestamps. A replay with the same `Idempotency-Key` and the same instants is the same entry.
5. **Unknown body fields are dropped, not rejected**, as in US-04.5-06. The controller spec asserts that a `status` field reaches no service.
6. **All-time is never reported as settlement-complete.** Coverage requires a bounded range.

## Found while implementing (fixed in this story's commits)

- The in-progress resume work wrote `purchase_scan` and `static_signal` attempts that the `0034` CHECK constraint rejected. `0034` was also missing from the Drizzle journal, so `db:migrate` would not have applied it.
- Account and settlement attempts were recorded before their findings. A crash between the two would have let a resumed run resolve a finding it never re-checked.
- The duplicate-provider signal paired `min(org_id)` with `min(purchase_id)` from different events, which could violate the tenant foreign key.
- Report-only inquiry still wrote purchase backoff state when the provider call threw.
- An identical settlement replay returned `409`, because stored timestamps came back in PostgreSQL text form and were compared as strings.
- Settlement history paged by `id` while ordering by `created_at`.
- Invalid-HMAC, callback-failure, callback-mismatch, and freeze paths did not emit `standalone-billing-alert`, so the documented Railway alerts could not fire.
- Frontend issues:
  - the settlement form minted a new `Idempotency-Key` on every submit, so retrying after a lost response created a duplicate;
  - the report-id field label was a missing translation key;
  - the run and settlement controls ignored the operator role;
  - the code filter stripped capitals instead of lowercasing;
  - Arabic last-run text was forced LTR and showed the raw run status;
  - the error state had no retry;
  - the run-trigger type said `scheduled` where the API sends `nightly`.
