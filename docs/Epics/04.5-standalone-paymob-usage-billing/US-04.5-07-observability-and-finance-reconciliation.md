## User story and value

As Akeed product, finance and operations, I want payment/credit reconciliation, alerts and commercial metrics, so that accounting defects are detected before merchants or revenue are materially affected.

**Business value:** Akeed can operate prepaid liability and conversion with measurable integrity rather than relying on support reports.

## Scope

Scheduled Paymob inquiry reconciliation, ledger/projection checks, settlement comparison inputs, anomaly queues, privacy-safe logs/metrics, operational alerts, product funnel and finance metrics, runbooks and retention/access rules.

**Out of scope:** Building a general data warehouse, automated accounting journal export, tax invoice engine, hard-coding Paymob fees, auto-resolving unmatched provider transactions or arbitrary partial refunds.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A retry-safe scheduled worker runs at least daily and around settlement for stale pending, successful, refunded, disputed or otherwise anomalous purchases using stored provider identifiers and bounded Paymob inquiry.
2. Reconciliation detects: provider success without purchase grant, grant without verified success, duplicate provider IDs, stale pending purchase, amount/currency/reference/integration mismatch, refund/dispute discrepancy, ledger/projection mismatch and settlement difference.
3. No unmatched provider result automatically grants credits. Discrepancies enter the staff queue with safe codes, first/last seen, retry count and next permitted action.
4. Ledger sum equals posted balance and held-reservation sum equals held projection are checked independently. Mismatch blocks account mutations, alerts and follows the audited repair workflow.
5. Alerts exist for invalid-HMAC spikes, trusted-field mismatch, callback/database failure, Paymob latency/error rate, stale pending, success-without-grant, projection mismatch, duplicate grant attempt, refund/chargeback/debt and reconciliation backlog age/size.
6. Structured logs contain request/internal references, sanitized provider IDs, transition names, hashes and error codes only; secrets, HMAC material, checkout client secrets, raw payloads, PAN/wallet/token and full phone/email are excluded.
7. Product metrics cover approved organizations, one-time grants, free-credit utilization, time to first accepted send, low/zero-balance reach, checkout starts/states/conversion, first and repeat purchase, purchase-size distribution and initial versus follow-up usage/reversal.
8. Finance metrics cover purchased credits, gross EGP, refunds, chargebacks, contracted fees input, net revenue, ARPPU, revenue per accepted message and unspent paid-credit liability without treating free credits as cash liability.
9. Metrics are organization-safe and deduplicated by immutable purchase/ledger source. Callback retries and repeated page views do not inflate revenue or conversion.
10. Runbooks define callback outage, Paymob API outage, stale pending, mismatched success, projection mismatch, refund/dispute, debt/support and credential rotation response with named evidence and disable controls.
11. Reconciliation and metrics jobs do not query or mutate Shopify subscriptions as Paymob facts; Shopify monitoring remains unchanged.

## Reconciliation policy

- Callbacks remain the primary real-time source; inquiry is recovery and comparison.
- Pending is not expired merely because local time elapsed. Inquiry must confirm no successful transaction.
- Provider success is applied only after the same field matching and idempotent transaction as callback processing.
- Settlement mismatch is visible to finance and does not independently alter merchant credits.
- A job retry uses stable reconciliation identities and exponential backoff with jitter/caps; poison anomalies stay visible rather than looping rapidly.
- Monitoring cardinality excludes raw organization/provider IDs from metric labels where that would be unbounded; searchable IDs stay in access-controlled logs/records.

## Implementation notes

- **Backend:** Add a BullMQ reconciliation job/processor and repository queries for bounded pages. Reuse the Paymob adapter through `PaymentsPort` and the same canonical transition service.
- **Frontend:** Extend the staff page with reconciliation queue, health summaries and safe business counters; a dedicated BI surface is not required.
- **Data:** Record attempts/results separately from canonical payment state. Define retention for sanitized events/logs without deleting immutable credit/purchase/audit evidence.
- **Security:** Job credentials remain server-only. Staff views keep AAL2 and do not expose customer payment method details beyond sanitized channel/type when needed.
- **Finance:** Fee/VAT/settlement calculations are configuration/imported facts from the signed merchant agreement, never guessed constants.

## Test requirements

- Scheduled job duplicate/enqueue/retry, pagination, timeout, rate limit, stale data and poison anomaly.
- Every discrepancy detector with positive/negative fixtures; matched success applies once, unmatched success never grants.
- Projection alert and mutation block; audited repair clears only after invariant passes.
- Metric deduplication under callback replay, multiple checkout views, refund/chargeback and free-credit use.
- Log/redaction assertions for known sensitive Paymob fields and application secrets.
- Runbook drills for callback/API/database/queue outage and credential rotation.
- Existing reliability monitoring and Shopify billing suites remain green.

## Migration and rollout

Run reconciliation in report-only mode against sandbox and the internal production organization. Compare dashboard/report totals manually before enabling canonical transition recovery. Turn on alerts with calibrated thresholds for the allowlisted cohort; retain a kill switch for active inquiry while keeping local invariant checks.
