## User story and value

As the Akeed product owner, I want reproducible evidence that billing, messaging and recovery work in sandbox and a controlled production cohort, so that paid access is not launched with duplicate grants, lost money or Shopify regressions.

**Business value:** Akeed launches an auditable revenue path with controlled financial, tenant and provider risk.

## Scope

Composed automated gate, disposable PostgreSQL contracts, Paymob card/wallet sandbox acceptance, controlled live card/Vodafone Cash payment, Meta consumption, refund/debt/dispute and reconciliation drills, settlement evidence, merchant/staff walkthroughs and go/no-go record.

**Out of scope:** Broad rollout, recurring subscriptions, saved cards, direct wallet integration, new pricing/tax decisions, performance claims beyond measured evidence or closing inherited external gates by assertion.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All US-04.5-01 through US-04.5-07 criteria have dated evidence tied to their implementation commits; no story is represented as complete solely because its document exists.
2. A disposable PostgreSQL gate applies the additive migration, validates constraints/RLS/immutability/tenant FKs, exercises concurrency and replays recovery, then proves forward/disable rollback without deleting financial history.
3. Automated tests cover approval/grant, purchase/idempotency/roles/tenant isolation, HMAC and trusted-field matching, callback order/replay/failure, usage transitions/generations, refunds/chargebacks/debt, invariant repair, reconciliation and both locales.
4. The complete E04 manual-order lifecycle, history, retry, duplicate submission and merchant acceptance baseline remains green. Existing Shopify plans, checkout, callback/webhook, periods, quotas, settings and entitlement contracts remain green.
5. Paymob sandbox completes card success, decline, cancel/abandonment, expiry, duplicate/delayed/out-of-order callback, database-failure retry, missing-callback inquiry and refund. Redirects are proven unable to add credits.
6. After Paymob enables the wallet integration, sandbox completes Vodafone Cash success/failure and captures processed callback/HMAC behavior. Akeed makes no direct Vodafone request and receives no wallet credential.
7. Owner/admin/viewer and staff AAL2 workflows pass in English/Arabic, LTR/RTL and keyboard navigation, including pending approval, low/zero/debt, purchase return, history, adjustment and reconciliation.
8. An internal production organization completes controlled real card and Vodafone Cash purchases; Paymob dashboard, Akeed purchase, callback event, ledger, balance and bank/settlement evidence reconcile exactly.
9. Controlled live Meta sends prove initial and follow-up acceptance consume separately, pre-accept rejection releases, delivery failure reverses, ambiguity holds, and last-credit concurrency permits one claim.
10. A controlled full/whole-credit refund and chargeback/dispute drill proves reversal/debt/block/reinstatement behavior. If Paymob cannot safely provide a live chargeback drill, documented provider evidence plus an isolated production-like simulation is recorded as an explicit residual gate, not silently passed.
11. Monitoring fires for injected HMAC, stale pending, success-without-grant, ledger mismatch, refund/debt and Paymob outage conditions; staff completes the corresponding runbook and clears the reconciliation queue.
12. Release approval records contracted fees/VAT/settlement/refund/chargeback handling, support ownership, feature/allowlist/kill-switch state, known limitations and the exact evidence reviewed.
13. No duplicate free/purchase/consumption/reversal entry, cross-tenant access, sensitive log value, unexplained projection mismatch or unresolved high-severity reconciliation item remains.

## Migration and rollout

1. Deploy additive schema and application with billing disabled.
2. Validate configuration, callback reachability/HMAC, inquiry, monitoring and report-only reconciliation with an internal organization.
3. Enable staff approval and one allowlisted internal account; reconcile its free grant and live sends.
4. Enable checkout for the internal account; run real card and Vodafone Cash purchase, callback retry, refund and settlement checks.
5. Add a small allowlisted merchant cohort with active support monitoring.
6. Expand only after the observation window has no ledger mismatch, duplicate grant, callback reliability breach, unexplained settlement discrepancy, isolation failure or unresolved severity-one/two item.

Rollback disables checkout and new Standalone holds/approvals through feature controls. It does not delete purchases, callbacks, grants, holds, ledger, audit, orders or history. In-flight callbacks remain accepted and reconciled so paid money is not orphaned.
