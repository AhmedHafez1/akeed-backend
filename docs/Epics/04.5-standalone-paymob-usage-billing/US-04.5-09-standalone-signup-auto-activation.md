## User story and value

As a new Standalone merchant, I want my account active with 30 free credits as soon as I verify my email, so that I can start confirming orders without waiting for Akeed staff.

**Business value:** removes a manual staff bottleneck from self-serve acquisition while keeping the launch grant auditable and exactly-once.

## Scope

Signup → email verification → account active → 30 free credits. Remove the staff approval requirement, its API, UI, copy, tests and database fields. Backfill accounts still pending.

**Out of scope:** phone verification, changing the Supabase email verification flow, and any Shopify onboarding, plan or billing behaviour.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Standalone provisioning, which requires a confirmed Supabase session, opens the credit account `active` and posts `free_grant +STANDALONE_FREE_GRANT` (default 30) in the same transaction as the organization and source, keyed `standalone-free-grant:<orgId>:v1` with the owner as actor.
2. Retried or concurrent provisioning for the same user never posts a second grant.
3. A user who owns a Shopify or other native source is still refused with `STANDALONE_SOURCE_CONFLICT`, and no credit account is opened.
4. Onboarding completion, manual orders, test sends and automatic sends no longer check for approval. A suspended account still blocks them, and a missing account reports `CREDIT_ACCOUNT_NOT_PROVISIONED`.
5. The staff approval routes (`/api/admin/standalone-billing/approvals/*`), `STANDALONE_CREDIT_APPROVAL_ENABLED`, and the approval preview/apply UI are removed. The staff account list, account detail and billing operations remain.
6. Migration `0035` activates every `pending_approval` account with one grant, aborts naming any conflicting organization, then drops the approval columns and the `pending_approval` status.
7. Merchant, admin and marketing copy in Arabic and English no longer describes a manual approval step.
8. Shopify contract, platform-boundary and E04.5 suites pass unchanged in behaviour.
