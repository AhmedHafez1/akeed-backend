## User story and value

As a Standalone merchant, I want credits charged only for messages Meta accepts and restored for confirmed failures, so that my balance reflects useful message attempts while concurrent workers cannot overspend it.

**Business value:** Prepaid access is fair, deterministic and safe under retries, callbacks and concurrency.

## Scope

Provider-neutral accounting routing, Standalone hold/consume/release/reverse transitions, initial and follow-up billing, ambiguous-send reconciliation, retry generations, credit-specific errors and zero/debt behavior.

**Out of scope:** Paymob, checkout, pricing UI, new Meta templates, redesigning verification lifecycle or changing Shopify monthly accounting.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `UsageAccountingRouter` chooses existing periodic-plan accounting for Shopify and prepaid-credit accounting for Standalone from the trusted integration platform; verification, orders and messaging do not know a payment provider.
2. In the dispatch-claim transaction, Standalone locks the account, verifies `active`, no debt and `posted_balance - held_credits >= 1`, creates one held reservation tied to dispatch/kind/generation, increments held credits and claims the dispatch atomically.
3. With one available credit and concurrent distinct sends, exactly one claim succeeds. Duplicate workers for the same logical dispatch reuse its reservation and never hold or post twice.
4. Meta acceptance with a returned provider message ID atomically projects the existing verification acceptance, posts `consumption -1`, marks the reservation consumed and decrements held credits. A duplicate acceptance is a no-op.
5. Every accepted initial and every accepted follow-up costs one separate credit. A no-reply transition without a sent message costs nothing.
6. A confirmed failure before provider acceptance releases the held reservation once and posts no consumption. An exception/missing ID or persistence ambiguity that cannot prove rejection stays `outcome_unknown` with the credit held for staff resolution.
7. A confirmed provider delivery failure after consumption posts one `failure_reversal +1`; duplicate/older failure callbacks do not restore twice. Existing accepted send facts and verification history remain visible.
8. Staff resolution of an ambiguous outcome as accepted consumes the existing hold; resolution as rejected releases it. Both paths are idempotent and fully audited.
9. A new generation can be created only after the preceding generation has a confirmed terminal rejection/failure and its reservation is released/reversed. Accepted or ambiguous generations cannot be resent. Generation is included in dispatch and billable identity.
10. Pending approval, suspended account, debt and insufficient available credits return respectively `STANDALONE_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`, `CREDIT_ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED`, `CREDIT_DEBT_OUTSTANDING` and `INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS` from the transactional claim. Advisory API/UI checks use the same vocabulary but are not the accounting authority.
11. Zero/debt prevents manual-order and verification-retry billable actions without hiding existing orders, details, outcomes, ledger or purchase history. Existing in-flight holds can complete after a later refund creates debt.
12. Shopify continues using current plan entitlement, `integration_monthly_usage`, period start/end, `plan_limit_reached`, reservation release and retry behavior with no credit-table access.

## Transaction rules

- Account row lock order is consistent across send, purchase, refund and adjustment paths to avoid deadlocks.
- `available` is computed inside the lock; cached/UI balances never authorize a send.
- The dispatch and reservation share org-scoped foreign keys. The reservation key is stable for `(verification, kind, generation)`.
- Posted balance changes only with a ledger insert in the same transaction. Holds change `held_credits` and reservation state but do not fabricate ledger consumption.
- A ledger conflict means “already applied”; code reloads the canonical state and does not repeat the projection mutation.
- Any ledger/projection/reservation mismatch fails closed with a reconciliation error rather than guessing a balance.

## Implementation notes

- **Backend:** Refactor `VerificationMessageDispatchesRepository.claim`, `markAccepted`, failed-provider handling, `recordProviderStatus` and `resolveNotAccepted` through the accounting contract. Preserve current verification projection and lease behavior.
- **Frontend:** Map credit denials on manual creation, test verification and retry actions. Keep list/detail/history enabled and link low/zero states to billing once US-04.5-05 lands.
- **Data:** Backfill generation 1. Add a new generation only from a locked prior terminal row; never mutate the identity of historical rows.
- **Security:** Derive account org from authenticated integration/dispatch relationships. Never accept an org, balance, cost or reservation state from a worker payload or browser.
- **Operations:** Existing `outcome_unknown` dispatches require a reviewed migration policy; do not release/consume them automatically without evidence.

## Test requirements

- Last-credit race, separate organizations, duplicate worker, expired lease reclaim and duplicate acceptance.
- Initial plus follow-up acceptance; no_reply; pre-accept confirmed rejection; exception/missing ID ambiguity; post-accept delivery failure and duplicate callback.
- Staff accepted/rejected resolution; new generation after confirmed failure; retry denial after accepted/ambiguous outcome.
- Refund-created debt while a hold exists, then hold acceptance/release and no new claims.
- Projection invariant after every transition and injected rollback between ledger/reservation/account/verification writes.
- E04 order lifecycle and duplicate-request suites remain green. Full Shopify entitlement/send/period regression proves no behavioral change.

## Migration and rollout

Ship the router dark, reconcile pending accounts and account projections, then enable for the same allowlisted approved Standalone cohort. Pause Standalone workers only for the compatibility cutover if required; Shopify workers remain active. Rollback disables new Standalone holds and preserves reservations/ledger for reconciliation.
