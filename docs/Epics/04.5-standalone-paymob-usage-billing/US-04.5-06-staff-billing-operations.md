## User story and value

As authorized Akeed staff, I want to inspect, reconcile and adjust a merchant’s credit/payment history with audit evidence, so that payment anomalies can be resolved without silently editing balances or trusting a browser redirect.

**Business value:** Support and finance can recover safely from provider, database, refund and dispute exceptions.

## Scope

Staff account search/detail, payment/event/reservation/ledger inspection, signed adjustments, ambiguous-send resolution, inquiry-triggered reconciliation, refund/chargeback recording, projection repair and audit trails.

**Out of scope:** Manually declaring an unverified payment successful, deleting ledger entries, editing provider identifiers, arbitrary refund automation, issuing Paymob refunds without separately approved provider permissions, or bypassing tenant/account invariants.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `/api/admin/standalone-billing` and its UI require existing staff authorization, production AAL2, feature flag and throttling; merchant organization roles confer no staff access.
2. Staff can filter accounts by approval, low/zero balance, debt, suspension and reconciliation state without exposing payment credentials or full customer contact data.
3. Organization detail reconciles account projection with immutable ledger, held reservations, purchases, provider-event summaries and admin audit history. Any mismatch is prominent and blocks mutations except audited repair.
4. Staff can preview/apply a signed nonzero credit adjustment with required reason and idempotency key. The account lock, ledger entry, projection and admin audit commit together; duplicate apply is a no-op.
5. Staff resolves `outcome_unknown` dispatches as accepted only with a provider message ID/evidence or as not accepted with a reason. The existing held credit is consumed/released exactly once and retry generation rules remain enforced.
6. Staff can trigger a Paymob inquiry for pending/anomalous purchase. Inquiry uses stored trusted identifiers and may apply the same verified state-transition rules, but staff cannot type/select “successful” or create a purchase grant manually.
7. Staff can record provider-confirmed refund/dispute evidence and affected minor amount. Exact whole-credit reversal, debt creation, chargeback open/lost reversal and won reinstatement use immutable idempotent ledger entries.
8. Non-exact partial refund, missing identifier, amount/currency mismatch or unsupported provider state stays quarantined with an explicit reconciliation reason and grants/reverses nothing automatically.
9. Projection repair recomputes posted balance from ledger and held count from reservation state under lock, records before/after/difference/reason/request/actor, and refuses repair if source rows remain internally contradictory.
10. Every read/write logs sanitized internal references, result/error code and request ID; no secret, HMAC, checkout client secret, PAN/wallet/token or full callback payload appears in API, audit metadata or logs.
11. Cross-tenant IDs cannot combine an account with another organization’s purchase, dispatch, reservation or ledger entry even for staff mutation endpoints.

## Staff interface

- `GET /api/admin/standalone-billing/accounts`
- `GET /api/admin/standalone-billing/accounts/:orgId`
- `POST /api/admin/standalone-billing/accounts/:orgId/adjustments/preview`
- `POST /api/admin/standalone-billing/accounts/:orgId/adjustments/apply`
- `POST /api/admin/standalone-billing/dispatches/:dispatchId/resolve`
- `POST /api/admin/standalone-billing/purchases/:purchaseRef/reconcile`
- `POST /api/admin/standalone-billing/purchases/:purchaseRef/provider-action`
- `POST /api/admin/standalone-billing/accounts/:orgId/projection-repair/preview`
- `POST /api/admin/standalone-billing/accounts/:orgId/projection-repair/apply`

Preview/apply pairs carry server-created fingerprints so changed state cannot be adjusted from a stale review. Reasons are trimmed, length-bounded and required. Provider-action input records evidence; it is not authority to assert provider success.

## Implementation notes

- **Backend:** Reuse `AdminAccessGuard`, `AdminAccessAuditRepository` and the existing message-dispatch resolver. Centralize ledger mutation; controllers remain thin.
- **Frontend:** Add a focused staff billing page within `AdminShell`, with safe filters, detail timelines, explicit preview confirmation and readable discrepancy states.
- **Data:** Account/purchase locks follow the same global order as callback and send paths. Adjustment/reversal keys include organization and the immutable operation source.
- **Security:** Staff access is broad for investigation but relationships remain tenant-consistent. Use authorization plus database constraints; never accept balance before/after from the staff browser.
- **Operations:** Document who may adjust, required evidence, dual-control policy if finance requires it, and escalation for negative balances/chargebacks.

## Test requirements

- Staff/nonstaff, AAL1/AAL2, flag, throttle and safe audit behavior.
- Account filters/detail and sanitized fields; deliberately conflicting cross-tenant IDs.
- Positive/negative adjustment, stale preview, duplicate/lost response, concurrency with send/purchase/refund.
- Ambiguous accepted/rejected resolution and retry generation effects.
- Inquiry success/failure/timeout/mismatch; prove no manual-success endpoint exists.
- Full/whole-credit refund, partial mismatch, chargeback open/lost/won and debt.
- Projection repair valid mismatch, contradictory source refusal and immutable ledger.
- Arabic/English admin status labels where the existing admin UI is localized; keyboard/confirmation behavior.

## Migration and rollout

Enable read-only inspection first, then approval/adjustment/reconciliation writes for named staff after a recovery drill. Keep all writes behind the feature flag. Rollback disables mutations and preserves the audit/ledger trail.
