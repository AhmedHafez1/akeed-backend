## Rollback record

Initial deployment defaults:

- `STANDALONE_CREDIT_BILLING_ENABLED=false`
- `STANDALONE_CREDIT_APPROVAL_ENABLED=false`
- `STANDALONE_BILLING_OPERATIONS_ENABLED=false`
- `STANDALONE_BILLING_SCHEDULED_INQUIRY_ENABLED=false`
- `STANDALONE_BILLING_RECONCILIATION_REPORT_ONLY=true`

Rollback disables new checkout, Standalone holds and approvals. It does not delete purchases, callbacks, grants, holds, ledger entries, audits, orders or history. In-flight callbacks remain accepted and reconciled so provider-confirmed money is not orphaned.

Before rollout, record the named owner for each switch, the internal organization allowlist, the observation window and the exact rollback execution time. Broad production rollout remains out of scope for this evidence pack.
